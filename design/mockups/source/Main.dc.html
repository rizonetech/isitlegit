Malicious · high confidence

This is a password-phishing email pretending to be NimbusMail.

The link leads to a lookalike login page that sends your password to someone else. Do not use it.

What to do now

  1. Do not click the link or open the attachment.
  2. If anyone typed a password on that page, change it now and sign out of all sessions.
  3. Delete the email and warn your team: it was sent to look like a routine NimbusMail notice.
