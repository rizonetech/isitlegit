Add a DNS TXT record

In your domain's DNS settings (for example at your registrar or Cloudflare), add this record. It changes nothing about your website or mail.

TypeTXT
Name_isitlegit.kcexpress.example
Valueisitlegit-verify=7f3c9a1e52d84b06
How do I add this at my provider?

Your DNS host is whoever your domain's nameservers point to, which is not always the company that sold you the domain. If someone else manages your DNS, send them the Name and Value above.

Cloudflare
  1. Open dash.cloudflare.com, choose your domain, then DNS and Records.
  2. Choose Add record. Type: TXT. Name: _isitlegit (Cloudflare adds your domain to it). Content: the value above. TTL: leave Auto. Save.
  3. Then come back and press Check now. New records can take up to an hour.
