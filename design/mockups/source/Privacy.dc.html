Who is responsible for your information?

Rizonetech (Pty) Ltd in George, Western Cape, runs IsItLegit and is the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Our Information Officer is Derick Payne, reachable at hello@isitlegit.co.za.

What do we process, and why?

  • Mail you forward or upload, with its attachments, links and any voice note: to investigate it and give you a verdict.
  • Your account: your name, work email, organisation and role, so you can sign in and see your cases.
  • Your supplier ledger: supplier names, domains and bank details you add, to catch changed bank accounts.
  • Feedback when you tell us a verdict was wrong, to measure and improve accuracy.

We accept mail only from members' verified domains, and we never use your mail to train a model.

What happens to a protected business's details?

A protected business's registered bank account is never shown to anyone who checks mail. A checker only learns whether an invoice "matches" or "does not match the registered account", and checks are rate-limited so the details cannot be guessed.

What are your rights?

You may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. Write to hello@isitlegit.co.za and we will answer. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Information Regulator.
