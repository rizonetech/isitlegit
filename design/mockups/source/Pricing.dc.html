Questions about pricing

Do I need a card to start?

No. There is no billing during the launch, so there is nothing to pay and no card to enter.

How do I join during the launch?

With an invite code. New organisations get a 14-day trial with every feature unlocked, so you can try Protect on your own mail.

What happens when the launch ends?

Billing has not started. When it does, you choose whether to move to a paid plan; the prices on this page are launch prices.

Why is checking free?

Because the people who receive a fake are the ones who catch it. Every check of mail that claims to be a protected business also protects that business.
