About and contact

Who is behind IsItLegit?

Rizonetech (Pty) Ltd, a managed IT provider in George, Western Cape. We built IsItLegit because our clients kept phoning us with the same question about a suspicious email, and the honest answer took a technician hours.

IsItLegit is a Rizonetech product. Source code is public at github.com/rizonetech/isitlegit under the FSL-1.1-ALv2 licence.

How was it built?

IsItLegit was built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon by Derick Payne, Rizonetech's CEO, working as a solo developer who directs a team of AI coding agents. Every section of the plan is reviewed by an independent model before it ships.

The investigation itself runs on Meta's models: Muse Spark reads and decides, Muse Wonder walks links, SAM 3.1 checks logos, and Muse Voice Transcribe listens to voice notes. We publish what each one adds on the results page.

Why start with small businesses?

Large companies have security teams. A courier, a supplier or a practice with ten people has whoever answers the phone. The scams that cost them most, changed bank details and fake proofs of payment, need a clear answer before money moves, not a filter setting.

The trust network grew from a real case at one of our clients, where a criminal pretended to be a supplier to that supplier's own courier.
