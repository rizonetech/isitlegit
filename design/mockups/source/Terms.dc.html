Who provides the service?

IsItLegit is run by Rizonetech (Pty) Ltd, George, South Africa. During the launch it is free, organisations join with an invite code, and each organisation has a daily limit on checks.

What does a verdict mean?

A verdict of safe, suspicious or malicious is our assessment of the evidence we could gather at that moment. It is not a guarantee. Attackers change tactics, links change after we check them, and a genuine mailbox can be taken over. Before you pay new bank details, phone the supplier on the number in your own records. Before you release goods, confirm the money has cleared in your own banking app.

What do we ask of you?

  • Forward or upload only mail you are allowed to share with a service provider.
  • Keep your sign-in to yourself, and remove team members who leave.
  • Do not use IsItLegit to test attacks on other people, to send mail, or to probe protected businesses' details.
  • Tell us when a verdict is wrong, using the button on the report.

What applies to protected businesses?

Register only domains, logos and bank accounts that belong to your business, and keep them current. Your seal and badge show Not verified when your protection lapses. We never contact registrars or hosting providers on your behalf; the takedown pack is yours to send.

What are the limits?

We aim to answer every check within a few minutes, but we cannot promise the service will always be available during the launch. When a model is unavailable or a daily cap is reached, you get a report marked partial that says which checks did not run.

Can these terms change?

Yes. We will date every change on this page and tell members by email before a change that affects them takes effect. These terms are governed by the law of South Africa. Questions go to hello@isitlegit.co.za.
