Why did we build this?

At one of our clients, a criminal emailed a supplier's courier while pretending to be that supplier and asked for its statement. The supplier's own mail filter never saw the email, because it never reached the supplier. We tell the story here with a fictional name: Fynbos Freight.

3 · Signature badge

A badge that turns copies into evidence

Add the badge to your email signature. Anyone can copy a picture, so the badge is not the proof: your mail's own SPF, DKIM and DMARC are, checked against your registered domains when a recipient forwards it. A fake that copies your badge becomes an impersonation verdict and an alert to you. If your protection lapses, the badge shows Not verified, even in old emails.

5 · Bank verification

Changed bank details get confirmed by you

When a customer checks an invoice in your name with different bank details, we ask your registered finance contact to confirm. Your account number is never revealed: a checker only learns "matches" or "does not match the registered account". Checks are rate-limited so nobody can guess their way to your details.
