Invoice fraud

What is invoice fraud?

An invoice arrives that looks exactly like your supplier's, but the bank account belongs to a criminal. You pay it, the real supplier is still owed, and the money is usually gone within hours.

The one rule that stops it

Before paying new bank details, phone the supplier on the number you already have. Never use the number in the email.

Is that proof of payment real?

SABRIC's 2025 report describes victims who were induced to "release goods based on falsified proof of payment" (page 9). Upload the PDF or screenshot on its own and we look for the signs of editing.

  • Edits saved after the document was created
  • Mixed fonts in the amount or account fields
  • Dates in the file that disagree with the page
  • A layout that differs from that bank's real notices

A check can show a document is likely doctored. It cannot prove that money was paid, so the report always says: confirm the money has cleared in your own banking app before you release goods.
