How does IsItLegit check an email?

Forward it as an attachment to check@isitlegit.co.za. We run eight checks and four Meta models over it, and in about a minute you get a verdict by email with the evidence behind every reason.

Why forward as an attachment?

A normal forward throws away the original headers, and the headers are where your own mail server recorded whether the sender is who they claim. Forwarding as an attachment keeps them. A normal forward still gets checked, but with less confidence, and the reply tells you how to send it properly next time.

Outlook on Windows

  1. Select the suspicious email in your inbox.
  2. Press Ctrl Alt F, or choose More, Forward as attachment.
  3. Send it to check@isitlegit.co.za.

Outlook on the web and new Outlook

  1. Open the email.
  2. Choose More actions, then Forward, then Forward as attachment.
  3. Send it to check@isitlegit.co.za.

Gmail

  1. Tick the email in your inbox list.
  2. Choose More, then Forward as attachment.
  3. Send it to check@isitlegit.co.za.

Only mail from members' verified domains is accepted. Anything else gets no reply, so the address cannot be used to send spam. You can also upload an .eml or .msg file, a PDF or a voice note once you sign in.

What happens to the email?

Every step produces a piece of evidence. The verdict may only cite evidence that exists, so nothing in your report is a guess.

Checks

Who really sent it

SPF, DKIM and DMARC as recorded by your own mail server, display name tricks, a different Reply-To, and whether anyone at your business has heard from this sender before.

Checks

Lookalike domains and links

Swapped letters and added words, how old the domain is, where every link really lands, QR codes in images, and whether Google Web Risk lists the exact link.

Checks

Attachments and hidden text

Files pretending to be something else, HTML smuggling, macros, and words hidden from you but aimed at AI filters. An honest sender has no reason to hide words from the reader.

Muse Spark

Reads it the way you see it

The email, its PDFs and its landing pages are rendered as pictures. Muse Spark says which brand it claims to be, what it wants you to do and how hard it pushes.

SAM 3.1

Checks the logos

SAM 3.1 cuts out every logo, stamp and signature. Each crop is compared with the real brand's logo, or with the logo a protected business registered.

Muse Wonder

Walks the link for you

In a sealed browser, Muse Wonder clicks through the lure the way a victim would and types made-up test values, never real ones, to show where a password would have gone.

Voice

Listens to voice notes

Muse Voice Transcribe turns a voice note into text, then Muse Spark pulls out the request and who the caller claims to be. It supports English but not Afrikaans, isiZulu or isiXhosa yet, and we do not claim to detect voice cloning.

Verdict

Weighs it and cites the proof

Muse Spark weighs all the evidence and writes the verdict. A changed bank account or a password form on a lookalike domain sets a floor the model cannot talk down.

What do you get back?

A reply to your forward with the verdict, the top three reasons and what to do now, plus a link to the full report. Report links are signed and expire after 7 days.

How long do we keep it?

The email and its attachments are deleted after 30 days. The case summary and evidence stay so your history keeps working. Privacy and POPIA

Does Meta keep my mail?

We call Meta's Model API with standard model IDs and store: false on every request, and never in the mode that allows training on what we send.
