# Loupe's public page datasets

Every public dataset considered for training Loupe, IsItLegit's page screener (D03 T04 §5), with its licence and the decision. Public facts only: no page content. A dataset is used only when its licence allows training a model for a commercial product; research-only, non-commercial, by-request and unclear terms are rejected (design spec 4.9). GPT-6 Astra researched and downloaded the candidates on 2026-10-28 under `docs/glimmer/astra-collection-brief.md`; Claude re-read each accepted licence at its source and re-checked every file's SHA-256 before importing.

Benign render lists (D03 T04 §6) are kept out of the table, since their pages are our own renders rather than a dataset's: the Majestic Million (https://majestic.com/reports/majestic-million, CC BY 3.0, by Majestic), downloaded 2026-10-28 from https://downloads.majestic.com/majestic_million.csv (1,000,000 domains), of which `raw/majestic/top.csv` keeps the top 20,000 and the 576 highest-ranked `.za` domains not among them (20,576 in all; widened from the top 5,000 on 2026-10-29 for Loupe 1.0, D03 T04 §18); and IsItLegit's own `eval/glimmer/benign-sa-urls.txt`. Majestic is credited in `NOTICE.md`. The widened render (D03 T04 §18) finished on 2026-10-30 at 09:42 SAST: every one of the 20,576 domains has an outcome (`raw/majestic/RENDER.json`, list SHA-256 `4f94be7b2573fc8de69015d4d0053ce9b4b163e628b6f789818eb25f673f16d3`): 8,366 homepages stored and 12,210 excluded, the largest reasons HTTP 403 (2,619), a render over 20 seconds (1,299), a cookie wall over the page (1,248), only part of the page shown (949), a page still changing while captured (372) and a bot challenge drawn over it (278); 9,529 page retries and 11 browser restarts along the way. It ran at 4 renders at a time except for one run at 6 (operator, 2026-10-29 20:23 to 22:48 SAST), which came out faster but with a third of its outcomes retries, so 4 stands: about 1,300 to 1,500 outcomes an hour at 4, 19.5 hours of rendering in all. `benign-sa-urls.txt` holds 662 URLs, 382 stored and 280 excluded. A recheck of all 8,742 stored benign pages on 2026-10-30 found none to remove, damaged or unassessed.

Label errors is the spot check's share of wrong labels in 50 random pages a source (`-` before it ran). Above 10 percent, the source's `lure` labels are marked `unverified` and go to adjudication instead of being trusted.

Inputs, receipts and labels (D03 T04 §18). Every accepted source's files are bound in `raw/<source>/INPUTS.json` before a converter reads them: a file is admitted against the digest its publisher gives (PhreshPhish's Hugging Face SHA-256 for each shard; 7 Days Later's Zenodo MD5, `c7ec2fd9f59510aeba745e1b85329fdf`) or, where the publisher gives none, against the immutable version it was pinned to (CompPhish, Mendeley DOI 10.17632/fmbs4kp9wz.4; Ariyadasa, Mendeley DOI 10.17632/n96ncsr5g4.1; Kempf, Kaggle version 1; Look-for-Mallory, Kaggle version 2; ScamFerret, Git commit 8cf8dc631edafe584141e2f95f0c0f51855fb7bc), with our SHA-256 taken at the first download. A file changed since, or with neither a digest nor a pin, never reaches conversion. Each converter writes `raw/<source>/EXPORT.json`, counting every item it met as exported, sampled out or rejected with its reason; each import's `IMPORT.json` names the manifest and the rows it was made from, and `node scripts/glimmer/sources.ts check` refuses an import made from anything else.

Label mappings. PhreshPhish, CompPhish, Ariyadasa and Kempf give `phish` or benign per page, taken as the page's `lure` (ground truth); PhreshPhish's target brand is its `brand`. Look-for-Mallory's `spam` and `ham` rows map to lure and not; a URL row is trusted only when its category matches the page's. 7 Days Later labels every crawled URL a lure, and its earliest capture that answered 200 with a screenshot and HTML is used. ScamFerret's scam categories map to lure types only where the category settles one. A publisher's label holds only on a capture of the page it was given for: a capture showing a parked or suspended domain, a takedown or error notice, or a bot wall is excluded, and one page text two sources label oppositely is excluded for both (`scripts/glimmer/label-rules.ts`). 7 Days Later's screenshot is its live capture while its text and form flags come from an inert copy of the saved HTML, so a form flag that reads false there is `unverified` and never evaluated.

Provenance. Each page keeps its dataset (`source`), its item id there (`item`) and its host; none of the 1.0 sources gives a campaign, kit or template id, so `template` stays empty for them, and §10 groups pages into one split by brand, else site, with exact and near-duplicate pages across sources removed before the split.
