> **Resolved:** 2026-10-30, round 2: P1 fixed. A greeting name proves nothing (RFC 5321 section 4.4), so no hop is judged by its `from` name any more. Under Microsoft 365 or Google (the receiver whose hop is at the top), the topmost of their own headers is read: they stamp every message on receipt, above anything a sender supplied, whatever the hops below claim. Under another receiver, a hop is internal only when the address the receiver recorded in its TCP information is private or loopback; the first hop from a public address is the entry, and only headers above it count. The spoofed-greeting fixture and the same trick against Microsoft 365 are regressions that fail with the private-address rule or the topmost rule removed.
