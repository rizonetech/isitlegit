For example, an ordinary short-link host is extracted, following times out before reaching the impersonating destination, and `skipped` remains empty. The check reports `ran` with “no lookalike of a known name” instead of `partial`. Expansion failures genuinely become `null` in [analysis-workflow.ts:624](../../../src/pipeline/analysis-workflow.ts#L624). Furthermore, comparison overflow emits `compared: false`, while [runner.ts:198](../../../src/checks/runner.ts#L198) automatically recognises only `checked: false`.

**P2: Redirect provenance cannot reliably identify the originating link.** [domain-lookalike.ts:158](../../../src/checks/domain-lookalike.ts#L158) records the hop URL and literal `part: "link"`, omitting `e.link`, the originating part, and chain position. Two independent links reaching the same suspicious host become indistinguishable. Additionally, `Expansion.hops` includes every main-document response, including a direct page returning 200 ([expand.ts:570](../../../src/checks/expand.ts#L570)); treating every entry as “behind a redirect” falsely asserts a redirect on a direct visit.

**P3: Provenance merging and persistence lose distinctions.** The duplicate key at [domain-lookalike.ts:139](../../../src/checks/domain-lookalike.ts#L139) ignores `part`, so retained link occurrences with different source parts collapse, incrementing `foundMore` even before five entries exist. URLs then pass through [auth.ts:198](../../../src/checks/auth.ts#L198), which clips ordinary strings to 255 characters. Distinct supported URLs sharing a long prefix can therefore become identical evidence. The persistence test checks only the domain, so it cannot detect either loss.

My strongest practical comparator is **Microsoft Defender for Office 365 with Safe Links and Threat Explorer**. It checks supported links during mail flow and at click time; its investigation records associate the clicked URL and redirect chain with message identity, time and detection method. [Safe Links documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-office-365/safe-links-about), [URL investigation schema](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-xdr/advanced-hunting-urlclickevents-table).

These limits are not intrinsically defects, but this candidate neither demonstrates comparable detection under them nor consistently reports their coverage loss.
