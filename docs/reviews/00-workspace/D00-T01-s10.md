**Part 2: excellence**

The strongest alternative is Cloudflare’s native Wrangler/dashboard rollback, combined with application smoke verification. Native rollback accepts a named version in one command, supports recovery from split deployments, and provides version selection in interactive mode. Gradual deployments let operators expose a fraction of traffic and compare errors before promotion. These are documented capabilities, not a measured speed benchmark. Sources: [Cloudflare rollbacks](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/versions-and-deployments/rollbacks/), [gradual deployments](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/versions-and-deployments/gradual-deployments/).

2. **in-scope, E2: Long waits lack incident-grade feedback.** History lookup, rollback and health propagation print only their initial stage message. They can remain silent for most of the four-minute budget, unlike this repository’s smoke command, which reports elapsed time every ten seconds. **Target:** progress at least every ten seconds during every waiting stage, with elapsed time and the last observed health state, plus total rollback duration.
