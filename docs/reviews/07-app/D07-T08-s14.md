**Part 2: excellence**

Comparison: **Microsoft account passkey management**, a relevant leading account-security alternative. Microsoft documents a native creation flow with selectable storage locations, and management showing storage location, last use, Rename and Remove. Its documented creation flow requires two account-level selections before the native ceremony; this candidate requires one Add selection, followed by naming and Save. I found no supported click-count disadvantage for the candidate. No comparative latency measurements were available. [Microsoft creation guide](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accounts-billing/security/create-save-passkey), [Microsoft management guide](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accounts-billing/security/manage-your-saved-passkeys).

1. **in-scope, E1: Progress feedback is insufficient.** Before the native prompt and while registration is saved, Add only receives `aria-busy`; its visible label remains unchanged and the message is cleared. A slow connection looks like an ignored click. [app.ts:1047](../../../src/web/client/app.ts#L1047). **Target:** visible and announced progress within 100 ms, distinguishing preparation from saving, with one ceremony under repeated activation. Benchmark click-to-prompt and post-confirmation-to-result on a representative mobile connection before claiming “a few seconds”.

2. **in-scope, E2: Recovery and naming prevent an excellence claim.** C1–C5 leave users uncertain which credential was added, whether a rename saved, or what name they kept. Microsoft’s documented identifiable credential entries provide the comparison baseline; its undocumented failure behaviour is not assumed superior. **Target:** zero incorrect credential associations, zero false “keep” labels, recovery without a second registration or blind rename resubmission, and direct focus on invalid input.

3. **out-of-scope, E3: Storage identity is weaker.** Microsoft identifies where a passkey is saved. This page supplies a user-entered label and generic device type, which cannot distinguish two synced providers or explain that a credential is not confined to one device. [settings-account.tsx:92](../../../src/web/pages/settings-account.tsx#L92). **Target:** reliable provider/authenticator identification where available, an honest unknown fallback, and clear synced-versus-device-bound wording. This needs a new **D07 account/authentication section and design approval**, rather than guessing from the browser. The accepted last-used deviation is not reopened.
