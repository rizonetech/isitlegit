1. **P2: Session renewal cookies are lost outside the successful member response.** [report.tsx:157](../../../src/web/pages/report.tsx#L157). Reproduce with a session approaching its original cookie expiry: open a valid signed report belonging to another organisation. `getSession` renews the database session, but the public response discards its `Set-Cookie`. Forward authentication cookies through every response after the session read, as `requireSession` does. [Better Auth renewal contract](https://better-auth.com/docs/concepts/session-management#session-expiration).

The user cannot activate B from this report’s switcher; organisation-scoped consumers still use A through `requireOrg`. Preserve the distinction between the report’s organisation and the session’s active organisation. [Better Auth organisation contract](https://better-auth.com/docs/plugins/organization).
