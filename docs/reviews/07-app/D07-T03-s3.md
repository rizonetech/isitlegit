Thus an accepted hostile SVG qualifies for inline delivery from the application’s origin. Specify verified passive image formats for inline responses; serve SVG and other active or mismatched content as inert downloads. Add route tests that persist an artefact, open the report as both a member and an anonymous signed-link holder, follow its generated URL, and verify the exact bytes and headers. Include expired and tampered signatures and a member opening with an expired link.

Test keyboard activation and focus on the revealed target, including direct fragment navigation.

5. **P2: Renderer coverage does not prove the evidence is displayed correctly.** [Section line 165](../../../todo/07-app/TODO-03-report-page.md#L165) can pass with family renderers that output only the common summary. One fixture per registered check also misses real variants: `domain.age` has both dates and unchecked-domain records; `content.hidden_text` has quotes, overflow counts and incomplete-scan records. `not_checked` is deliberately absent from `checks.names()`.
