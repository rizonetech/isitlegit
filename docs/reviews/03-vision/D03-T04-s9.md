1. **P1, §9 lines 365 and 372: the US$20 cap does not cover every attempt.** `sparkAsk()` permits client retries, but `teach()` reserves one local entry and settles only the final answer’s cost. A 503 followed by success loses the first attempt’s uncertain spend; refusal of the retry settles the whole call at zero. The local estimate also budgets 400 output tokens while the request permits 600. Require per-attempt reservations against the Glimmer cap using the client’s estimates and validated costs. Test retry-success and retry-refusal sequences near US$20.

2. **P2, §9 lines 365–366: resume is not crash-safe.** In `teacher-label.ts:178–207`, payment settlement precedes the checked record, and the checked record precedes queueing and label updates. A stop can therefore cause another paid call, permanently lose a disagreement, or leave missing fields unrepaired. Require durable per-page attempt/result state, temp-file-then-replace publication, and recovery before sending again. Test interruption at each boundary, including a partial journal write.

3. **P2, §9 lines 360 and 364–365: incomplete pages have no guaranteed terminal outcome.** The rules only derive types for `lure: true`; sample imports can store `lure: false` without `lure_type`, and the teacher never fills that field. Pages with no source `lure`, including conflicting sample labels, are skipped entirely. Require an exhaustive final inventory: every page validates as a complete answer or has an exclusion entry. Add benign `lure_type: none` completion and tests for missing and conflicting labels.

4. **P2, §9 lines 365 and 377: failed or absent teacher checks still permit evaluation.** Invalid JSON, invalid answers and transport failures become `answered: false` but are permanently marked checked without exclusion. `build-dataset.ts` never consults checked outcomes, so those pages, and pages beyond the default 500-call batch or a budget stop, remain evaluation-eligible. Require explicit pending, answered, failed and refused states, with §10 admitting only successfully checked evaluation pages. Test each failure and an interrupted or capped batch without requiring extra spend.

5. **P2, §9 lines 364–365: input integrity is not an acceptance gate.** Both rules and teacher read stored bytes without comparing their full SHA-256 hashes; `readPage()` does not verify them. The teacher also silently omits malformed metadata through `manifest({ tolerant: true })`. Require verification before deriving labels or sending requests, with damaged records accounted for and excluded. Test altered text, a substituted valid JPEG and malformed metadata.

6. **P2, §9 line 365: rule-derived brands can corrupt labels and held-out grouping.** `derivedBrand()` takes the first recognised name or a hostname stem, and treats an unrecognised brand as no brand. Consequently, a genuine `standardbank.co.za` page becomes `standardbank`, while its lure can become `Standard Bank`; §10 groups those separately, allowing the same brand into training and evaluation. Require canonical brand grouping, a distinction between unresolved and genuinely absent brands, and tests for aliases, third-party brand mentions and named brands outside the dictionary. Preserve original source labels.

7. **P2, §9 lines 360–372 and 377: no real-store completion receipt or recorded handoff is required.** The checkpoint can pass without running either script on the store. Both commands print counts, but §9 requires no persisted, reconciled report for §10. Add a verified run receipt recording the input snapshot, completed labels, exclusions, teacher outcomes, remaining work and measured/uncertain spend, with figures recorded in `docs/glimmer/training.md`. Require read-back and a failing completion check for unexplained omissions or incomplete execution.

8. **P3, §9 lines 372 and 377: the SAST-date requirement lacks proof and is currently violated.** `teach()` captures a UTC date once at startup, while `localBudget()` uses the current SAST day per attempt. Require an injected clock and assertions around SAST midnight, including a run crossing midnight, proving that local entries and guard records use the same accounting day.
