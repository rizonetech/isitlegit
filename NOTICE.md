## Reference logos (not in the repository)

Real brands' logos are held as detection references only, in the private R2 store `ref/logos/` of the `isitlegit-artefacts` bucket, never in this repository, never served to anyone and never imitated on IsItLegit's pages (design spec section 11, Fixtures; D03 T03 §2). Each is its owner's trademark, rendered from the header of the owner's own website by `scripts/load-reference-logos.ts`, and is used only to recognise a mail or page that impersonates that owner. The store's index records, for every logo, the page and source it was taken from, the date and `use: "detection-reference"`; a logo whose page is not on one of its brand's own domains is refused.
